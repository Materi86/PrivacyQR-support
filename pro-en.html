Before you upgrade.

What can I do without Pro?

Create and save up to five QR codes for websites, text, Wi-Fi and contact cards. Scanning and custom design require Pro.

Do I need an internet connection?

QR tools work locally. Pro purchases, restoration and entitlement updates may require internet access through Apple. Opening links or sharing to external services may also use a network.

Can I revoke a shared QR code?

No. Anyone who scans a shared image can read its content. Code locks, Kill Switch and expiry only apply inside PrivacyQR and cannot revoke shared, printed or photographed images.
